Residential renovations.

Kitchens, bathrooms and living spaces renovated to update the layout, the look and the way a home works.

Overview

New life for lived-in homes.

Renovation projects breathe new life into tired homes. You can update the layout to create better spaces, make structural changes, or simply modernise what already exists.

These residential interiors show what that looks like in practice: warm living spaces with feature walls, granite-topped kitchens, and bathrooms finished in large-format tile.
