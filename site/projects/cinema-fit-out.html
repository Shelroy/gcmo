Case study

Cinema & entertainment fit-out.

A large commercial interior taken from open steel shell to finished cinema: lobby, concession stands, auditoriums and the plant rooms behind them.

Overview

A complete interior, start to finish.

This project took an open steel building shell and turned it into a finished cinema: a lobby with sweeping curved ceilings, concession counters, upper-level circulation, auditoriums with tiered seating, and the power and mechanical rooms that keep it all running.

It brought together several GCMC services on one site, coordinated by one team from structure to final finishes.

  • General construction
  • Construction management
  • Interior fit-out
  • Mechanical & electrical

The build

From shell to showtime.

How the space came together, stage by stage.

  1. 01

    Structure & services

    Structural steel and overhead services were set out in the open building shell.

  2. 02

    Framing

    Steel stud walls went up with insulation, along with the framework for the lobby's sweeping curved ceilings.

  3. 03

    Drywall & ceilings

    The curved bulkheads were boarded and finished, and suspended ceiling grids were installed.

  4. 04

    Finishes & fit-out

    Floors, feature colours, lighting and the concession counters went in.

  5. 05

    Ready to open

    A finished lobby and auditoriums, ready for the first audience.
