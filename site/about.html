About GCMC

Over a decade of trusted experience.

A general contracting and construction management firm in Georgetown, Guyana, with a boutique approach and top-down involvement on every project.

Our story

One of the Caribbean's trusted contractors.

With over a decade of trusted experience, GCMC has grown to become one of the top general contracting and construction management firms in the Caribbean.

The cornerstones of our business are solid construction and excellence in on-site management. We bring our clients the expertise and full capabilities of a large firm, while keeping a boutique approach with top-down involvement.

That means our teams work together on every project, small and large, from principals and directors to project managers, estimators, supervisors and our labourers on site.

"However large the team, you will always have one contact making sure your project needs are met."

We have the ability to assemble a custom team to tackle any project, large or small. We understand the needs of business owners, contractors, brokers, developers and landlords, and we build our team around yours.
